# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Added Emerald, Ruby and Sapphire support, including Dive and surfacing.
- Added Fly to the PokéNav map while preserving browsing and zoom.
- Confirmed PokéNav flights now close START automatically before takeoff.
- Integrated Fly into the Pokégear Map Card in Gold, Silver and Crystal and
  removed the duplicate MAP entry from START.
- Added explicit A-to-enter and B-to-return Pokégear navigation. FREE cursor
  movement works in all four directions after entering the map.
- Fixed music remaining silent after browsing an untuned Pokégear radio frequency.
- Kept ordinary LIGHT separate from Emerald's Registeel Flash puzzle and
  preserved Ruby/Sapphire's original Regi puzzle requirements.
- Improved field-move stability and cleanup without changing move requirements
  or story progression.

## 1.2.0

- Added FireRed and LeafGreen support, including Rock Smash.
- Integrated Fly into TOWN MAP with location labels, landmarks and available
  Sevii map pages. Fly confirmation stays over the open map.
- Added CROSS-REGION FLY for travel between already available Kanto and Sevii
  destinations after the mandatory Sevii story sequence.
- Simplified FIELD MOVE USER to GENERIC and KNOWN MOVE across all supported
  games. Existing FIRST PARTY preferences switch to KNOWN MOVE.
- GENERIC and KNOWN MOVE fallback hide Pokémon identity and intros. A Pokémon
  that knows the move retains the game's original presentation.
- Fixed CONFIRM PROMPTS for Cut/Surf from the Gen 1 Pokémon menu and contextual
  field moves from the Gen 2 Pokémon menu.
- Show MAP CURSOR only in Gen 1/2 and CROSS-REGION FLY only in FireRed/LeafGreen.
- Fixed TOWN MAP becoming unresponsive after selecting a Fly destination in FRLG.

## 1.1.2

- Improved field-move stability while preserving existing HM/badge requirements
  and gameplay behavior.

## 1.1.1

- Fixed missing Kanto location labels and Fly selection with the FREE cursor
  in Crystal saves without a Hall of Fame entry.

## 1.1.0

- Added FREE and CLASSIC map cursor modes.

## 1.0.0

- Added Gold and Silver support alongside Red, Blue, Yellow and Crystal.
